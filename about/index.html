About this wall

This gallery archives all-time top image posts from r/mathmemes as a Mathera / Wayfinder-adjacent bonus: a constellation of memes we can later map onto skills and age bands.

What this is (and isn’t)

Is: a research shelf — how much of a curriculum could be glimpsed “in meme form,” and which jokes land at which stage.

Isn’t: a curriculum map yet. We have not tagged memes to Mathera skills in this build. That pass comes later.

Sorting & sources

Reddit tiles are sorted by score (upvotes) descending. Julius’s personal Memes (math) Photos album is included as personal tiles (no fake upvote count — they wear a personal badge and sort after Reddit). Images are stored locally (not hotlinked). Reddit metadata came from the public Arctic Shift archive API. Reddit tiles credit u/author and link the permalink; personal tiles credit Julius.

Age notes

Posts Reddit marks over_18 are included in this personal archive, marked with nsfw:true in JSON and an NSFW badge/border on the tile. A Show NSFW toggle is present (default ON here) so a future public build can hide them.

Takedown

Authors who want a post removed: open an issue on the GitHub repo with the post id or permalink. We will remove the local copy promptly.
